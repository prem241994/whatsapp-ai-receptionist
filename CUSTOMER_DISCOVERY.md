# Customer Discovery & Salon Pilot Playbook

## Hypotheses to test
H1: Salon owners lose bookings because messages go unanswered or staff are busy.  
H2: Owners will connect an official WhatsApp integration if setup is simple and they retain control.  
H3: Automated replies, reliable bookings, and reminders create measurable value.  
H4: Owners will pay a monthly subscription after a successful pilot.

## Ideal first customer
Independently operated Bengaluru salon with 1–10 staff, appointment-based services, regular WhatsApp enquiries, owner decision-maker, and no deeply entrenched booking software. Avoid claiming any salon is interested without interviewing it.

## Finding and contacting salons
1. Research public business listings on Google Maps, salon websites and Instagram business pages in HSR Layout, Koramangala, Jayanagar and Indiranagar.
2. Create a lead sheet: business, neighborhood, public contact channel, decision-maker (if voluntarily provided), existing booking method, outreach status, next action, interview findings.
3. Prefer a short phone call, in-person visit or requested email; do not scrape private contacts or send unsolicited bulk WhatsApp campaigns.
4. Ask permission to follow up and record consent appropriately.
5. Aim for 15 discovery conversations, then 3 demos, then 2 opt-in pilots. These are goals, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Initial outreach script
"Hi, I'm building a simple WhatsApp appointment assistant for local salons. I'm interviewing a few owners about how they manage enquiries and bookings. Would you have 10 minutes to share how it works at your salon? I'm not selling a subscription today."

## Interview questions (ask about actual past behavior)
1. How do customers currently book? Calls, WhatsApp, walk-ins, apps?
2. Roughly how many WhatsApp enquiries do you receive daily?
3. Who responds when staff are busy or after closing?
4. Can you recall a recent missed enquiry or double booking?
5. How do you manage staff schedules, holidays and walk-ins?
6. How often do people cancel, reschedule or not show up?
7. Do you currently send reminders? How much time does that take?
8. What booking/CRM software are you paying for now?
9. Would you trust an assistant to reply automatically? What must it never do?
10. If a working demo solved your biggest problem, would you pilot it? Would you pay for it afterward? At what price?

## Pilot design
- Written agreement defining scope, pilot length, responsibilities, opt-in, data handling, fallback and cancellation.
- Pilot only with an owner-authorized business WhatsApp account and consenting participants.
- Start with FAQs + assisted booking; add fully automated booking after reliability tests.
- Owner can disable bot and take over conversations instantly.
- No marketing broadcasts in MVP.
- Track: enquiry response time, booking completion rate, number of human interventions, incorrect answers, failed/duplicate bookings, reminders delivered, opt-outs, estimated bookings saved, support burden.
- Review weekly with the business owner.

## Validation gates
- Discovery: at least 10 interviews, evidence of a recurring pain, 2+ owners willing to pilot.
- Product: no known cross-tenant data leaks, no duplicate bookings in concurrency tests, reliable handoff and fallback.
- Commercial: at least 2 completed pilots, explicit willingness to pay, measured cost per business and realistic support effort.
- If signals are weak, change niche or feature scope before building more.

## Pricing experiments (not verified market prices)
Try ₹499 starter / ₹999 growth / ₹1,999 business with message and AI usage limits, or a setup fee where onboarding is labor-intensive. Calculate actual Meta messaging, AI, hosting, support, taxes and payment processing before publishing prices. Do not promise unlimited use.
